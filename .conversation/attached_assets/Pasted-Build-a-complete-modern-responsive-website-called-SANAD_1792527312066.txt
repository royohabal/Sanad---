Build a complete, modern, responsive website called SANAD (سند) that allows customers to tip workers digitally by scanning a QR code. The goal is to help workers who miss out on tips because people no longer carry cash.
The website should feel like a real, professional FinTech startup designed for the Kuwaiti market. Prioritize simplicity, trust, accessibility, and a smooth user experience.
Create a polished, modern design that feels welcoming, trustworthy, and distinctive.

Customers must be able to tip workers without creating an account or signing in.
The flow should be extremely simple:
1. Scan the QR code on a worker's badge.
2. Open a dedicated tipping page showing the worker's name, photo, and company.
3. Choose a suggested tip amount or enter a custom amount in KWD.
4. Optionally leave a rating.
5. Pay through the configured payment gateway.
6. See a clear payment confirmation.
Requirements:
- Aim for a tipping process that takes less than 10 seconds before payment, excluding the payment provider's authentication steps.
- Keep the page focused on the worker, tip amount, and payment button.
- No mandatory customer registration, reviews, comments, or personal information.
- Display the total and currency clearly before payment.
- Show clear loading, success, failure, and cancelled-payment states.
- Verify payment status securely on the server before recording a successful tip.
- Never mark a payment successful based only on a redirect to a success page.
Each worker should have a secure personal account showing:
- Total tips earned.
- Pending tips.
- Tips already paid through payroll.
- This month's earnings.
- Recent transactions.
- Monthly earnings chart.
- Average customer rating.
- Downloadable earnings statement.
- Personal QR code and printable badge.
Use large financial figures, clear labels, and simple charts instead of long explanations.
Workers should be able to access their accounts using a secure invitation and an authentication method supported by the configured authentication provider. Investigate phone-number OTP support before implementing it; do not assume it is enabled.
Companies should have a dedicated dashboard where they can:
- Register and manage their company profile.
- Add, edit, activate, and deactivate workers.
- Invite workers to access their accounts.
- Generate and download individual QR badges.
- Organize workers by branch or department.
- View individual and company-wide tipping totals.
- Review transaction history.
- Manage monthly tip payout records.
- Distinguish pending payouts from completed payouts.
- Generate monthly reports and export CSV files.
The interface should be easy to understand, even for a manager who is not technically experienced.
Treat each worker's wallet as an earnings ledger unless a properly supported regulated wallet provider is configured.
Clearly distinguish:
- Tips successfully received through the payment gateway.
- Tips awaiting settlement or payroll processing.
- Tips recorded as paid by the employer.
- Payment processing fees, refunds, and failed transactions where applicable.
Implement secure authorization so workers can only access their own records and company users can only manage their authorized company's workers.
Use server-side payment verification, unique transaction references, duplicate-payment protection, input validation, and audit logs for important financial changes.
Never expose payment secrets or sensitive environment variables in frontend code. Do not use simulated payments in production or claim that money has been transferred when only a record has been created.
